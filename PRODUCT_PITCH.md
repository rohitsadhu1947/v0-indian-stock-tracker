## 📊 How It Works

### 1. **Master Scoring System** (Patent-able?)

Every stock gets a score out of 100:

```
Total Score = Technical (40) + Fundamental (30) + Intelligence (30)
```

**Technical (40 points):**
- Momentum indicators: RSI, MACD, moving averages
- Breakout patterns: Price breaking key levels
- Volume surge: Institutional interest
- Trend strength: EMA alignment

**Fundamental (30 points):**
- **Blue Chip (30)**: Market cap > 50K Cr, ROE > 15%, P/E 5-30, D/E < 5
- **Quality (20)**: Market cap > 10K Cr, ROE > 10%, P/E 5-40, D/E < 10
- **Speculative (10)**: Market cap > 1K Cr, ROE > 5%, P/E < 50

**Intelligence (30 points):**
- Analyst ratings: Strong Buy (15), Buy (12), Hold (6)
- Target upside: > 15% (10 points), > 10% (8), > 5% (5)
- News sentiment: Bullish (5), Neutral (3), Bearish (1)

### 2. **Signal Categorization**

- **HIGH CONVICTION (85-100)**: 🔥 All 3 factors strong - "Put real money here"
- **MODERATE (75-84)**: ✅ 2-3 factors good - "Solid opportunity"
- **WATCH (60-74)**: 👀 Decent setup, monitor - "Wait for confirmation"
- **SKIP (<60)**: ❌ Not ready

### 3. **Automated Trade Planning**

For every signal, system calculates:
- **Entry price**: Current market price or next candle open
- **Stop-loss**: Technical support or 2.5% default
- **Target 1**: First resistance or 4% reward
- **Target 2**: Second resistance or 8% reward
- **Position size**: Based on max 2% risk per trade
- **Capital required**: Entry price × Position size

### 4. **Paper Trading & Backtesting**

- Execute trades without real money
- Auto P&L updates (every 5 mins during market hours)
- Auto-exit when stop-loss or targets hit
- Track win rate, total P&L, capital deployed
- Filter by open/closed positions
