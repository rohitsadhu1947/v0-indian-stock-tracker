## 📊 Understanding Trading Signals

### Signal Types Explained:

#### 🟢 STRONG BUY
**Meaning:** Multiple bullish indicators confirm uptrend.

**Indicators aligned (4+):**
- ✅ RSI < 40 (oversold, ready to bounce)
- ✅ MACD bullish crossover
- ✅ Price at lower Bollinger Band
- ✅ Above key moving averages
- ✅ High volume confirmation

**Action:** Strong buy candidate. Consider entering.

#### 🟢 BUY
**Meaning:** 2-3 bullish indicators, good but not perfect.

**Action:** Good buy candidate. Wait for better entry or enter with smaller position.

#### 🟡 HOLD
**Meaning:** Mixed signals or insufficient data.

**Action:** Don't enter. Wait for clearer signal. If you own it, hold.

#### 🔴 SELL
**Meaning:** 2-3 bearish indicators suggest downtrend.

**Action:** Consider exiting long positions or shorting (for experts).

#### 🔴 STRONG SELL
**Meaning:** Multiple bearish indicators confirm downtrend.

**Action:** Exit immediately if you own it. Strong short candidate (for experts).

## 📈 Technical Indicators Explained

### 1. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
**What it measures:** Is stock overbought or oversold?

**Range:** 0-100
- **< 30**: Oversold (may bounce up soon) → **BUY signal**
- **30-70**: Normal range
- **> 70**: Overbought (may fall soon) → **SELL signal**

**Example:** RELIANCE RSI = 25
- **Meaning:** Stock has fallen too much, likely to bounce
- **Action:** Look for BUY entry

### 2. MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
**What it measures:** Momentum and trend direction

**Signals:**
- **MACD > Signal Line**: Bullish (upward momentum) → **BUY**
- **MACD < Signal Line**: Bearish (downward momentum) → **SELL**
- **Histogram growing**: Momentum increasing
- **Histogram shrinking**: Momentum slowing

**Example:** MACD = 5.2, Signal = 3.1
- **Meaning:** MACD above signal = bullish
- **Action:** Uptrend confirmed, safe to buy

### 3. Bollinger Bands
**What it measures:** Volatility and overbought/oversold levels

**Signals:**
- **Price at Lower Band**: Oversold → **BUY**
- **Price at Upper Band**: Overbought → **SELL**
- **Bands widening**: High volatility
- **Bands narrowing**: Low volatility (breakout coming!)

**Example:** RELIANCE = ₹1450, Lower Band = ₹1445, Upper = ₹1550
- **Meaning:** Price near lower band = oversold
- **Action:** Good buy opportunity

### 4. Moving Averages (SMA20, SMA50)
**What it measures:** Average price over time (trend)

**Signals:**
- **Golden Cross** (SMA20 > SMA50): Strong uptrend → **BUY**
- **Death Cross** (SMA20 < SMA50): Strong downtrend → **SELL**
- **Price > SMA**: Uptrend
- **Price < SMA**: Downtrend

**Example:** TCS = ₹3100, SMA20 = ₹3050, SMA50 = ₹2980
- **Meaning:** Price above both MAs + Golden Cross = strong uptrend
- **Action:** Safe to buy with trend

### 5. Volume
**What it measures:** How many shares are trading

**Signals:**
- **High Volume + Price Up**: Strong buying → **Bullish**
- **High Volume + Price Down**: Strong selling → **Bearish**
- **Low Volume**: Weak move, ignore
- **Volume > 1.5x Average**: Institutional activity!

**Example:** INFY volume = 15M, Average = 8M
- **Meaning:** 1.88x average = strong interest
- **Action:** Move is confirmed by volume

## 📅 Daily Trading Workflow

### 🌅 MORNING ROUTINE (9:00-9:30 AM)

**Step 1: Market Prep** (9:00 AM)
1. Open app → Universe tab
2. Click "🔍 SCAN UNIVERSE (MORNING)"
3. Wait 2-3 minutes for results

**Step 2: Review Signals** (9:05 AM)
1. Look for **STRONG_BUY** signals with:
   - Success Rate > 50%
   - Confidence > 60%
2. Note top 5-10 stocks

**Step 3: Add to Watchlist** (9:10 AM)
1. Click "ADD" for top picks
2. Add to "Intraday Watchlist"
3. Prepare for market open

**Step 4: Market Open** (9:15 AM)
1. **If trading ORB**: Capture Opening Range (9:15-9:30)
2. **If using Morning Screener**: Run screener after 9:25 AM
3. Watch your watchlist for entry setups

---

### 📊 DURING MARKET (9:30 AM - 3:15 PM)

**Continuous Monitoring:**
1. **Position Tracker**: Enable "Auto-Refresh"
2. Watch for exit signals:
   - 🎯 Target Hit → Take profit immediately
   - 🛑 Stop Loss → Exit to prevent bigger loss
   - ⏰ Time Stop → Close before 3:15 PM

**Entry Rules:**
- Only enter if signal is STRONG
- Always set stop-loss BEFORE entering
- Never risk more than 1-2% of capital per trade

**Exit Rules:**
- Exit at target or stop-loss
- Don't get greedy!
- **CLOSE ALL POSITIONS by 3:15 PM** (intraday rule)

---

### 🌙 EVENING ROUTINE (After 3:30 PM)

**Step 1: Close Positions** (Before 3:30 PM!)
- Close ALL intraday positions
- Calculate total P&L for the day

**Step 2: Market Close Analysis** (After 3:30 PM)
1. Universe tab → "🌙 MARKET CLOSE ANALYSIS"
2. Wait 2-3 minutes
3. Review results:
   - **Market Sentiment**: What was today's mood?
   - **Sector Strength**: Which sectors led?
   - **Tomorrow's Theme**: What to focus on tomorrow?

**Step 3: Plan Tomorrow** (Evening)
1. Note "Top Picks for Tomorrow"
2. Set alerts if needed
3. Prepare mental stop-losses and targets

**Step 4: Next Morning** (9:00 AM)
1. Universe tab → "📥 LOAD YESTERDAY'S PICKS"
2. Loads evening's top picks
3. Run fresh morning scan
4. Compare both and decide!

## ❓ FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need a Zerodha account?**  
A: Yes, you need a Zerodha Kite account to use this app for real data.

**Q: Does this app place trades automatically?**  
A: No! This app only provides signals. YOU decide when to buy/sell.

**Q: How accurate are the signals?**  
A: Signals are based on technical analysis with 60 days of historical data. Success rate varies by stock (shown in Universe scanner). No signal is 100% accurate!

**Q: Can beginners use this?**  
A: Yes! Start with Watchlist tab. Add 5-10 stocks and learn by observing signals.

**Q: What if I don't know which stocks to trade?**  
A: Use **Universe Scanner** (morning)! It scans 500 stocks and recommends the best ones.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why does Watchlist show "HOLD" for all stocks?**  
A: Either:
1. Mixed technical signals (wait for clearer setup)
2. Session expired (re-login)
3. Weekend/holiday (no live data)

**Q: What's the difference between Screener and Universe?**  
A:
- **Screener**: Scans YOUR custom list (10-50 stocks)
- **Universe**: Scans entire NIFTY 500 (500 stocks)

**Q: Why are there 3 screener levels (Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive)?**  
A:
- **Conservative**: Strict rules, fewer signals, safer
- **Moderate**: Balanced (recommended for most)
- **Aggressive**: More signals, higher risk, for experienced traders

**Q: What does "Insufficient data" mean?**  
A: Not enough historical data to calculate indicators. Usually happens for:
- Very new stocks (IPO)
- Stocks with trading suspension
- Session expired (re-login!)

---

### Intraday Questions

**Q: What is intraday trading?**  
A: Buying and selling stocks on the SAME day. You don't hold overnight.

**Q: Do I HAVE to close positions by 3:15 PM?**  
A: For intraday (day trading), YES! Otherwise it becomes delivery and you'll need more margin.

**Q: What is ORB (Opening Range Breakout)?**  
A: A strategy where you:
1. Note high/low of first 15 min (9:15-9:30)
2. When price breaks above high → BUY
3. When price breaks below low → SELL (short)

**Q: What is Trade Watch for?**  
A: You add stocks you're interested in, and system monitors them 24/7. When perfect entry conditions appear (4-5 out of 5 indicators), you get an alert!

---

### Data & Privacy Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes! We only access read-only market data. We NEVER access your funds or place trades.

**Q: How often does data refresh?**  
A: 
- Manual: Click "Refresh Prices"
- Auto: Enable "Auto-Refresh" (Position Tracker)
- Real-time during market hours

**Q: Why do I need to re-login every day?**  
A: Zerodha Kite sessions expire after ~7-8 hours for security.

## 🎓 Trading Concepts 101

### What is Technical Analysis?
Using price charts, indicators, and patterns to predict future price movement.

### Key Principles:

1. **Trend is Your Friend**
   - Don't fight the trend
   - Buy in uptrends, sell in downtrends

2. **Support & Resistance**
   - Support = price level where buying comes in
   - Resistance = price level where selling comes in

3. **Volume Confirms Price**
   - Price up + high volume = strong move
   - Price up + low volume = weak move

4. **Multiple Timeframe Analysis**
   - Check daily for trend
   - Check hourly for entry
   - Check 15-min for precision
