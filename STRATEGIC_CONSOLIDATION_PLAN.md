## 📋 **DETAILED PLAN:**

### **Phase 1: Create Master Scoring System (1-2 days)**

**Goal:** Build a 0-100 score for every stock that combines ALL signals

**Formula:**
```
MASTER SCORE = (Technical × 0.40) + (Fundamentals × 0.30) + (Intelligence × 0.30)

Where:
- Technical (40%):
  - Momentum score (RSI, MACD, breakout)
  - Volume surge
  - Price action pattern
  
- Fundamentals (30%):
  - P/E ratio (vs sector avg)
  - ROE, Debt/Equity
  - Earnings growth
  
- Intelligence (30%):
  - Analyst rating (BUY/HOLD/SELL)
  - Target price upside
  - News sentiment
  - Report recency
```

**Output:** Every stock gets a score like:
- 90-100: 🔥 STRONG BUY (1-2 per day)
- 80-89: ✅ BUY (3-5 per day)
- 70-79: 👀 WATCH (on radar)
- Below 70: ❌ Skip
