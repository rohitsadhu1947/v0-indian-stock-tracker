## 📊 Technical Indicators Cheat Sheet

|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **RSI** | < 30 (Oversold) | > 70 (Overbought) |
| **MACD** | Above Signal Line | Below Signal Line |

| **Moving Averages** | Golden Cross (SMA20 > SMA50) | Death Cross (SMA20 < SMA50) |
| **Volume** | High volume + Price up | High volume + Price down |

## 🎯 Intraday Trading Rules

### ✅ DO's
- ✅ Set stop-loss BEFORE entering
- ✅ Close all positions by 3:15 PM
- ✅ Risk only 1-2% per trade
- ✅ Use high-volume stocks only
- ✅ Follow your trading plan
- ✅ Take partial profits
- ✅ Keep a trading journal

### ❌ DON'Ts
- ❌ Hold overnight (intraday = same day!)
- ❌ Trade without stop-loss
- ❌ Revenge trade after loss
- ❌ Over-leverage
- ❌ Trade on emotions
- ❌ Ignore exit signals
- ❌ Risk more than 2% per trade

- [ ] Signal is STRONG BUY or BUY
- [ ] Volume > 1.5x average

- [ ] Position size calculated (1-2% risk)

## 🚨 Emergency Rules

### If Losing Money:
1. **Stop trading immediately**
2. Review what went wrong
3. Paper trade for a week
4. Return with smaller size

### If System Down:
1. Note your positions on paper
2. Use Kite app directly to close
3. Don't panic!

### If Session Expired:
1. Re-login immediately
2. Check open positions first
3. Resume trading

## 💰 Position Sizing Formula

**Safe Position Size:**
```
Risk per trade = 1-2% of capital
Position Size = Risk Amount / (Entry - Stop Loss)
```

**Example:**
- Capital: ₹1,00,000
- Risk: 2% = ₹2,000
- Entry: ₹1,000
- Stop Loss: ₹980
- Risk per share: ₹20
- Position Size: ₹2,000 / ₹20 = **100 shares**

## 🎓 Beginner's Roadmap

### Week 1-2: **Learn**
- Add 5 stocks to watchlist
- Observe signals daily
- Don't trade yet!
- Read USER_GUIDE.md

### Week 3-4: **Paper Trade**
- Note signals in diary
- Track "virtual" trades
- Calculate what P&L would be
- Learn from mistakes (no money lost!)

### Month 2: **Small Trades**
- Start with ₹5,000-10,000 capital
- 1-2 trades per day maximum
- Focus on learning, not earning
- Keep detailed journal

### Month 3+: **Scale Up**
- Increase size gradually
- Refine your strategy
- Track statistics
- Build consistency
